# Messaging brand assets

Original symbols are preserved, scaled without recolouring, displayed alongside their brand names. They identify the selected communication channel and do not imply endorsement.

- WhatsApp: original green symbol preview served by the official Meta WhatsApp brand resource page, https://www.meta.com/en-gb/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/ . Asset https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/yQ/r/iu_mCuZziJB.webp . The original WebP bytes are embedded unchanged in an SVG image wrapper for self-hosting.
- Viber: original Viber Icon Flat Purple.svg from Rakuten Viber Logo 2026, downloaded via https://www.viber.com/en/brand-center/ at https://www.viber.com/app/uploads/Rakuten-Viber-Logos-2026.zip . The original SVG file is unchanged.

No third-party requests are made to load these icons in production. The site hosts the two files locally.
