# Messaging brand assets

Original symbols are preserved, scaled without recolouring, displayed alongside their brand names. They identify the selected communication channel and do not imply endorsement.

- WhatsApp: scalable vector brand glyph shared with inonda.site, displayed in WhatsApp green (#25D366) and white. The prior raster wrapper is replaced so the symbol stays clear at button sizes.
- Viber: original Viber Icon Flat Purple.svg from Rakuten Viber Logo 2026, downloaded via https://www.viber.com/en/brand-center/ at https://www.viber.com/app/uploads/Rakuten-Viber-Logos-2026.zip . The original SVG file is unchanged.

No third-party requests are made to load these icons in production. The site hosts the two files locally.

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube use the standard brand SVG symbols from Font Awesome Free 7.3.1, https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/7.x/svgs/brands (facebook-f.svg, instagram.svg, tiktok.svg, youtube.svg). Original SVG geometry and attribution/license comments are retained. Fonticons, Inc.; icons CC BY 4.0: https://fontawesome.com/license/free . Brand symbols retain their attribution and use Facebook blue (#0866FF), Instagram’s multicolour gradient, TikTok black/cyan/red (#000000/#25F4EE/#FE2C55), and YouTube red (#FF0000). They are displayed on white tiles without colour filters.
