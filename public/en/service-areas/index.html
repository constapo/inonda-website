For small repairs estimated at up to €1,000, the on-site quotation visit is free within 10 km of our office in Strovolos and costs €50 beyond 10 km. In Limassol, Larnaca, Agia Napa and Paphos, quotation visits for small repairs up to €1,000 are charged; for work over €1,000, the quotation is included in the work. We assess, quote and take on only jobs of more than one day of work, whether repairs or any other work; jobs that take only a few minutes or hours are not quoted or undertaken.
