Property management in Larnaca

From city apartments to family houses in the surrounding villages, Inonda LTD helps Larnaca owners keep their properties maintained, rented and documented.

The Larnaca rental market

Close to the island's main airport and with a growing number of new developments, Larnaca attracts long-term residents, families and seasonal visitors. Properties near the seafront are also popular as holiday rentals.

What we do for Larnaca owners

  • maintenance and repairs, including preventive AC and water-heater servicing
  • property inspections with dated photos and written summaries
  • tenant communication and owner reporting
  • regular checks for vacant and holiday homes.
