Managing a Cyprus property from abroad

Many owners of apartments and holiday homes in Cyprus live in the UK, Greece, Israel, Ukraine, Romania or elsewhere. Here is how to keep your property safe, rented and stress-free from a distance.

Updated: 2026-10-03

The main challenges

When you are not on the island, small problems become harder: a leak no one notices, a tenant who cannot reach you, bills that go unpaid, or a holiday home that sits closed and damp all winter.

What to set up

  • A trusted local contact who holds keys and can reach the property within the hour.
  • Bills on direct debit: electricity (EAC), water, internet and the building's common expenses.
  • A Cyprus tax number and an accountant if you earn rental income on the island.
  • Insurance that covers a property you rent out or leave empty for part of the year.
  • An emergency plan: who to call for leaks, power cuts or storm damage.

Caring for a vacant or holiday home

  • regular visits to air the property and run the taps
  • checks after heavy rain or strong winds
  • AC and solar water heater servicing before the season
  • garden, pool and pest control where relevant
  • preparing the home before you or your guests arrive.

Staying informed

Ask for written reports with photos after every visit or repair, and for estimates before any significant spending. Messaging apps such as WhatsApp or Viber let you approve work from anywhere.

How a property manager helps

A local manager becomes your eyes and hands in Cyprus: handling tenants, coordinating contractors, carrying out inspections and maintenance, and keeping you updated. Inonda LTD speaks Greek, English, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew and Romanian.

Living abroad? Contact Inonda LTD by WhatsApp, Viber or email and tell us about your property.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent out my Cyprus property while living abroad?

Yes. Many owners do. You will need to declare the rental income in Cyprus, and most owners use a local property manager to handle tenants, repairs and inspections.

Who looks after my holiday home in Cyprus when I am away?

A property manager or key holder can visit on a schedule, check for leaks and storm damage, service the AC and water heater and prepare the home before you arrive.

This guide is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules in Cyprus change, so confirm the details for your own property with a lawyer or accountant.
