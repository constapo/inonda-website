Property management in Nicosia

Inonda LTD is based in Nicosia. We manage apartments and houses across the capital and its suburbs for local owners, investors and owners living abroad.

The Nicosia rental market

As the capital, Nicosia has steady year-round demand from professionals, civil servants, families and university students. Apartments close to the universities, the city centre and the business districts find tenants fast, while houses in the suburbs attract longer family tenancies.

What we do for Nicosia owners

  • fast maintenance and repairs with vetted local contractors
  • move-in, routine and move-out inspections with photo reports
  • tenant communication and regular owner updates
  • preventive maintenance: AC servicing before summer, gutters before winter.

Because our team is in Nicosia, we reach most properties in the city and suburbs the same day when something needs attention.
