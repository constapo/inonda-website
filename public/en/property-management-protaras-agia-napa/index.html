Property management in Protaras and Agia Napa

Holiday homes and seasonal rentals on the east coast need regular care while the owner is away. Inonda LTD looks after properties in Protaras, Agia Napa and the surrounding area.

Caring for holiday homes

Many properties here are used only part of the year or let to holidaymakers in summer. Closed homes can suffer from damp, leaks, dust and storm damage, and seaside air is hard on AC units and outdoor fittings.

What we do for east-coast owners

  • regular vacant-home checks, and an extra check after every storm
  • AC, solar water heater, pool and garden maintenance before the season
  • inspections before and after each rental period
  • preparing the property before you or your guests arrive.

If you let the property to holidaymakers, you need to register it with the Deputy Ministry of Tourism. See our landlord's guide.
