Maintenance and repairs

SERVICE DETAIL

We coordinate every aspect of property upkeep so small issues are resolved before they become costly. This includes:

  • 24/7 tenant issue reporting through phone, WhatsApp or email
  • a vetted network of plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, painters and general handymen
  • upfront cost estimates and owner approval for any repair above an agreed threshold
  • scheduled preventive maintenance (boiler servicing, gutter clearing, pest control, AC servicing) to reduce emergency call-outs
  • emergency response coordination for urgent issues such as leaks or electrical faults
  • a digital maintenance log so owners can see what was fixed, when, and at what cost
  • and warranty tracking on recent repairs and appliances
Benefit to owners: fewer emergency repairs, lower long-term maintenance costs, better tenant retention, and full visibility into every job carried out on your property.
