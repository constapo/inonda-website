Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Inonda LTD wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use a screen reader, only a keyboard, voice control or screen magnification. This statement covers www.inonda.site.

Standard we follow

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. This is the level required by the European standard EN 301 549, which is used to apply the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and Cyprus Law 57(I)/2024 on the accessibility requirements of products and services.

Current status

We last reviewed this website on 5 November 2026, using automated testing (axe-core) and manual checks of keyboard use, colour contrast and media. We are not aware of any content that does not meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. Automated tools cannot find every problem, so please tell us if something does not work for you.

What we have done

  • Pages are designed to work with a keyboard, and a link at the top of each page leads straight to the main content.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet the WCAG contrast ratios, and links inside text are underlined.
  • The website is available in Greek, English, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew (right to left) and Romanian.
  • The company presentation video has no speech. It has captions that say so, and a text version of everything shown on screen.

Content from other providers

Some features come from other providers, such as review widgets and links to WhatsApp, Viber and social networks. We choose them with accessibility in mind, but we do not control them.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website is hard to use, or you need information in another format, contact us:

  • Email: inonda2026@outlook.com
  • Phone or WhatsApp: +357 94 091 616 or +357 94 091 613

Please tell us the page address and what went wrong. We will reply and, where we can, fix the problem or give you the information another way.

If you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the competent authorities in Cyprus that enforce Law 57(I)/2024.

This statement was prepared on 5 November 2026.
