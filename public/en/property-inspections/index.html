Property inspections

SERVICE DETAIL

Regular inspections protect your investment and give you documented proof of your property's condition over time. Our process includes:

  • a detailed move-in inspection with dated photos and a signed condition report agreed with the tenant
  • routine inspections at agreed intervals to check for maintenance needs, lease compliance and general upkeep
  • a move-out inspection compared against the move-in report to fairly assess any deposit deductions
  • early identification of issues such as damp, pest activity or unauthorized alterations
  • and a written inspection summary sent to the owner after every visit
Benefit to owners: fewer disputes over deposits, early detection of costly problems, and a documented history of your property's condition.
