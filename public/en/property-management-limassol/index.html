The Limassol rental market

Limassol attracts international companies, relocating professionals and their families, which keeps demand for quality rentals high. Tenants here often expect well-maintained, furnished homes and quick responses to problems, so reliable management earns its fee here.

What we do for Limassol owners

  • coordinated maintenance and repairs with cost approval before work starts
  • documented inspections at move-in, during the tenancy and at move-out
  • clear communication with international tenants in several languages
  • regular reports, so owners living abroad always know what is happening.
