ABOUT INONDA LTD

Inonda LTD is a Nicosia-based property management company serving landlords, investors, homeowners and tenants across Cyprus. We handle the day-to-day work of managing a rental property — tenant communication, maintenance, inspections and reporting — so owners can enjoy the benefits of property investment without the daily demands.
